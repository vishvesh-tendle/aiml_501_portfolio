About me

I started in computer science and spent my early career turning health-sector data into decisions: building reports, finding patterns in patient and operational data, and explaining the results to people who weren't data specialists.

I'm now pursuing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Indiana Wesleyan University, with the goal of becoming a financial data analyst. I'm especially interested in how machine learning can be applied to risk, fraud, and forecasting in a way that is accurate, explainable, and responsible.

This portfolio reflects how I like to work: understand the method deeply, then communicate it clearly enough that anyone can follow it.
